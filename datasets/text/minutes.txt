City Council Meeting Minutes — March 12, 2025

Attendance: Mayor Lopez, Councilors Reed, Okonkwo, Patel, and Nguyen.
The council approved Ordinance 441 to expand the downtown bike lane network.
Budget amendment BA-19 allocated $250,000 for park renovations at Riverside Green.
The next meeting is scheduled for March 26, 2025 at 7:00 PM.
